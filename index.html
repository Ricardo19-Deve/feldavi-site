Security and privacy

No Feldavi servers. Nothing to leak.

Your data travels only between the iPhone and your company's Salesforce. We never receive it, store it or share it.

Salesforce sign-in

OAuth with PKCE on Salesforce's own page. Feldavi never sees passwords; SSO and MFA apply.

Your permissions, enforced

Salesforce checks object, field and record access on every request.

Encrypted on the device

The offline copy is encrypted with a key that never leaves the iPhone, and deleted at sign-out.

No tracking

No ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Salesforce best practices

Built the Salesforce way.

The app and the package follow Salesforce's security and development guidelines, so Feldavi fits the way your org is already governed.

OAuth 2.0 with PKCE

Sign-in through an External Client App. No passwords or client secrets on the device; you can revoke sessions in Salesforce.

Your permissions, enforced

Standard Salesforce APIs as the signed-in user. Apex runs with sharing, user-mode queries and stripInaccessible.

Permission sets, not profiles

Feldavi Admin and Feldavi User permission sets, plus a custom permission for configuration changes.

Secrets stay in Salesforce

AI keys in a protected custom setting, called through Remote Site Settings, never sent to devices.

Standard data model

Tasks, Events, Files, record types, dependent picklists, multi-currency and validation rules. No copies of your data.

Tested and scanned

34 Apex tests with 94% code coverage (75% required). Salesforce Code Analyzer reports no security findings.

Light on your limits

Location search with SOQL DISTANCE, paging, cached metadata and selective queries keep API usage low.

AppExchange-ready

API version 64.0, Lightning Web Components with SLDS 2, prepared for a managed package and the AppExchange security review.

For Salesforce admins

Live in an afternoon.

Install the Feldavi package, choose what reps see, and share a QR code. Each rep subscribes to Feldavi Pro in the app, monthly or yearly, through the App Store.

  1. Install the Feldavi package

    Adds Feldavi Setup, the mobile configuration API and permission sets to your org.

  2. Choose objects and fields

    Hidden, read only or editable, per field. Branding, AI and features in one place.

  3. Share the QR code

    Reps scan it in the app and sign in with their own Salesforce user.
